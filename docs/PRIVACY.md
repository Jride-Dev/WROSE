# WROSE Sentinel Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

## 1. Overview

WROSE Sentinel is a Reddit-native moderator support tool for the WROSE project, short for Working Reddit Operational Signal Engine.

This Privacy Policy explains what information WROSE Sentinel may process, how that information is used, and what the app does not do.

WROSE Sentinel is designed for moderator-support workflows and operational community awareness. It is not designed for individual surveillance, user profiling, advertising, or data resale.

## 2. Relationship to Reddit

WROSE Sentinel operates on Reddit through Reddit-approved developer tools and interfaces.

Use of WROSE Sentinel is also subject to Reddit's own policies, including Reddit's User Agreement, Reddit Privacy Policy, Reddit Developer Terms, Reddit Developer Data Protection Addendum, Devvit Rules, and applicable subreddit rules.

Reddit's own Privacy Policy explains how Reddit collects, uses, and shares information when people use Reddit services.

## 3. Information WROSE Sentinel May Process

Depending on app permissions, Reddit platform behavior, and moderator actions, WROSE Sentinel may process limited Reddit-related information such as:

- subreddit name
- post identifiers
- comment identifiers
- thread context
- post or comment text available through permitted Reddit access
- timestamps
- public activity metadata
- calculated operational signal summaries
- volatility or activity indicators
- moderator-requested analysis results

WROSE Sentinel is designed to analyze thread-level and subreddit-level operational patterns, not personal user profiles.

## 4. Information WROSE Sentinel Does Not Intentionally Collect

WROSE Sentinel does not intentionally collect:

- real names
- email addresses
- phone numbers
- payment information
- street addresses
- precise location data
- government identification numbers
- private messages
- passwords
- private account credentials

WROSE Sentinel does not sell personal information.

## 5. How Information Is Used

WROSE Sentinel may use permitted Reddit data to:

- analyze thread activity
- detect activity spikes
- summarize volatility signals
- generate replay or timeline indicators
- provide moderator-facing operational summaries
- help moderators understand community activity patterns

Outputs are informational only.

WROSE Sentinel does not replace moderator judgment.

## 6. No Automated Moderation Actions

WROSE Sentinel does not automatically:

- remove posts
- remove comments
- lock threads
- ban users
- mute users
- report users
- approve content
- distinguish content
- modify Reddit content

Every analysis response is expected to preserve:

`automated_action_taken: false`

## 7. No Individual Profiling

WROSE Sentinel is not designed to build personal user profiles.

The app does not claim to determine:

- user intent
- truthfulness
- political beliefs
- mental health status
- criminal risk
- future behavior

Any signal generated by WROSE Sentinel should be treated as an operational indicator, not a factual judgment about a person.

## 8. Data Storage

During development and testing, WROSE may store limited operational data in a local or project-controlled database for analysis and validation.

Stored data may include:

- subreddit records
- post records
- comment records
- signal snapshots
- anomaly records
- replay frames

Development storage is intended for testing, debugging, and improving the tool.

Before any public launch, storage behavior should be reviewed and documented for the deployed environment.

## 9. Data Sharing

WROSE Sentinel does not sell Reddit data.

WROSE Sentinel does not share personal profiles with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

Data may be processed through infrastructure necessary to operate the app, such as Reddit's Devvit platform, approved backend services, hosting providers, logging systems, or repositories used for development.

## 10. Data Retention

During development, test data may be retained as needed for debugging, validation, and project history.

Before public launch, WROSE Sentinel should define a clear retention policy for any deployed database or backend storage.

Moderators or communities may request that test or stored operational data be removed where technically practical.

## 11. Security

WROSE Sentinel uses a limited-permission design and avoids destructive moderation actions.

However, no software system can be guaranteed completely secure.

The project may rely on third-party packages, Reddit APIs, Devvit tooling, hosting providers, and other dependencies. Known dependency risks should be documented and reviewed before public release.

## 12. Children's Privacy

WROSE Sentinel is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

The app operates within Reddit's platform and Reddit's own age and account rules apply.

## 13. International Users

Reddit communities may include users from different regions.

WROSE Sentinel is currently an early-stage development project and is not designed as a jurisdiction-specific compliance system.

Before public launch or broad distribution, privacy obligations should be reviewed for applicable laws and platform requirements.

## 14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as WROSE Sentinel evolves.

The latest version should be maintained in the WROSE repository or another public project page.

Continued use of WROSE Sentinel after changes means you accept the updated policy.
